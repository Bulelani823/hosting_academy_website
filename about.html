Information About Us

Welcome to Pawsitive Pet Academy! We are dedicated to providing the best pet care and training services within this evergrowing pet community. We will make sure that our pet care education experience will be something you will never forget.

Our mission is to be able to promote the well-being of pets and their owners through education, training, and compassionate care. Our goal with this organisation is to make a positive impact on the lives of pets and their families. We make sure that we are able to bring large amounts of pet awareness to many different communities which will help to curb the amount of animal cruelty.

Our team is made up of highly skilled and passionate individuals who are dedicated to providing the best possible care and education for pets and their owners. We are committed to creating a positive and supportive environment for both pets and their owners, and we strive to make a difference in the lives of those we serve.
